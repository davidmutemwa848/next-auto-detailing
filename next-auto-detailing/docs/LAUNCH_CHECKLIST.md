# Launch checklist

- Confirm legal company details, contact details, opening hours and approved service list/prices.
- Add extracted, approved photographs and optimized H.264 hero video to `frontend/public/media`.
- Add real service/gallery records and admin management endpoints.
- Complete reset-token/email verification flows and CSRF token support.
- Finish booking creation, staff scheduling/assignment, quotation/invoice/receipt PDF generation, payment recording and payroll workflows.
- Confirm VAT status and obtain professional review of Zambian payroll/tax rules.
- Configure provider credentials and private media storage.
- Test role boundaries, ownership, database migrations, backups and restore.
- Run frontend build, backend tests, accessibility checks and security review.
- Verify deployed HTTPS, CORS, security headers, robots/sitemap and portal noindex policy.
